// Intelligence Layer

What the AI Reads

🔊
Speech Confidence

Detects trembling tone, rising inflections, and vocal hesitations that signal nervousness under pressure.

⚡
Filler Words

Tracks "um", "uh", "like", "you know" — and flags excessive use that undermines professional credibility.

👁️
Eye Contact

Computer vision measures where your gaze lands. Consistent eye contact signals confidence and trustworthiness.

🧍
Posture & Presence

Slouching, fidgeting, and closed body language are detected and factored into your total presence score.

🎯
Answer Relevance

NLP checks whether your answer actually addresses what was asked — or if you drifted off-topic under stress.

📈
Adaptive Difficulty

Questions get harder as you improve. The AI doesn't let you coast — it pushes until it finds your ceiling.

⏱️
Pacing Analysis

Too fast and you seem rehearsed. Too slow and you seem uncertain. The AI finds your optimal speaking pace.

🗂️
Structure Scoring

STAR method compliance, logical flow, and whether your answers have a clear beginning, middle, and end.
